The 30-second version

Habit Mosaic doesn't ask for an account and doesn't sync your habits to a cloud. Everything you track — habits, streaks, notes, moods — stays on your phone, and we never receive it.

The free version is supported by ads from Google AdMob, and the app uses PostHog for anonymous usage analytics you can switch off any time in Settings → Privacy. Habit Mosaic Premium removes all ads.

1. Information we process

1.1Stored only on your device — we never see it

Everything you create inside Habit Mosaic is saved to a local database on your device. We do not operate servers that hold this data:

  • Habit names, categories, icons, colors, reminder times
  • Daily check-ins and streak history
  • Mood entries and day notes
  • App settings and theme preferences
Implication: If you uninstall the app or wipe your device without exporting a JSON backup first, this data cannot be recovered — by us or anyone else.

1.2Advertising (free version only)

The free version of Habit Mosaic shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Formats include banners, interstitials between actions, rewarded ads you choose to watch, and an ad when the app opens.

Data Google receivesPurpose
Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement
Coarse device & app info (model, OS version, app version, language)Ad compatibility and reporting
IP addressApproximate location for relevance, fraud prevention

We never send Google your habits, notes, moods, or any content you create. Purchasing Premium removes all ads — and with them, this entire data flow.

Google acts as an independent controller for data collected through AdMob, governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalisation on your device at any time — Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads · iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

1.3Usage analytics

Habit Mosaic uses PostHog to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. This is on by default and can be switched off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data. Turning it off stops all further collection.

Events are identified by a random ID generated on your device — not your name, email, or account. We collect:

  • Lifecycle events: app opened, app backgrounded, install, update
  • Feature events such as habit_created, habit_completed, mood_set, theme_changed, language_changed, backup_exported, paywall_shown, purchase_completed
  • Whether you have an active subscription
Never collected: habit names, descriptions, day notes, mood text, or reminder times. Events record that something happened — never what it said.

PostHog, Inc. processes this on its United States infrastructure. Their policy: posthog.com/privacy

1.5If you email us

If you write to skyloopapps@gmail.com for support, feedback, or a privacy request, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and keep a record. You will not be added to any marketing list.

1.6Backups you export

Exporting a backup produces a JSON file containing your habits and history. You choose where it goes — a share sheet, cloud drive, email, or local storage. Once it leaves the app it is governed by the privacy policy of wherever you sent it. We never receive a copy.

1.7Diagnostics & crash data

We protect the limited information we do process with industry-standard measures, including HTTPS / TLS in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but because Habit Mosaic is local-first, the surface area for a breach on our side is intentionally minimal — your habits and notes are simply not on our servers to be breached.
